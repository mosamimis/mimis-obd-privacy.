Mimis OBD - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

On this page

Mimis OBD (“the app”) is a vehicle dashboard that reads data from your car through a Bluetooth or Wi-Fi OBD-II (ELM327) adapter. This policy explains what the app does with information. The app has no user accounts and does not require registration. There is no Mimis OBD server; the developer does not operate a backend that receives your data.

Information stored only on your device

The following is saved locally on your phone and is not uploaded to the developer or anyone else:

  • App settings (your chosen dashboard name label, car, theme, units, Wi-Fi adapter address, fuel level).
  • The last GPS position (latitude and longitude). The app keeps your most recent coordinates on the device so it can re-center the map and fetch local weather. They are stored only for this purpose.
  • Trip history (your last trips: time, speed, GPS speed, gear, RPM, temperature, fuel, and any fault codes seen). Trip history does not store your route or raw coordinates.
  • Fault-code history (diagnostic trouble codes with the date and time they were seen).
  • Notifications and alerts.
  • Drive recordings used for playback.
  • Vehicle information read from the adapter (for example speed, RPM, temperatures, fuel data, and, where your car reports them, the VIN and calibration IDs). This is used to display and diagnose, and is kept on the device.
  • An optional photo of your car and an optional custom 3D model. These are processed on the device (including background removal) and stored on the device; they are not transmitted.

This locally stored information stays on your device unless you explicitly export or share it (see “Exporting your data” below).

Information sent off your device

The app transmits data off your device only in these two cases, and only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection:

  • Google Maps: when a map is shown, your precise location (latitude and longitude) is sent to Google to draw the map and center it on you, using the Google Maps Embed API.
  • Weather (Open-Meteo): your location coordinates (rounded to about 100 metres) are sent to the Open-Meteo weather service (api.open-meteo.com) to show the local temperature in the app’s top bar.

No other information is transmitted automatically. Location is used only while you are using the app; the app does not request or use background location.

Third-party services

  • Google Maps Platform - your location is processed by Google when a map is displayed, under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Maps Platform Terms.
  • Open-Meteo - receives coordinates to return local weather. See Open-Meteo’s terms.

Exporting your data

Trip history, fault codes, recordings, and vehicle information leave your device only when you choose to. If you tap “Export troubleshooting report” or “Create repair report (PDF)”, the app opens your phone’s standard share or print screen so you can send or save the report to a destination you pick. The app does not send these reports anywhere on its own.

Advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking or crash-reporting software.

Permissions

  • Location (foreground only) - map position, GPS speed, and local weather.
  • Bluetooth - connect to your OBD-II adapter.
  • Notifications - the coolant-overheat alarm and the “running” notice while connected.
  • Network/Wi-Fi state - load the map and weather, and use a Wi-Fi OBD adapter.

How to delete your information

Because the app stores information only on your device, you control it directly:

  • Reset the current trip from the Trip page.
  • Clear fault-code history and clear notifications from the Alerts screen.
  • Delete saved drive recordings in Settings.
  • Clear the app’s data in Android Settings → Apps → Mimis OBD → Storage, or uninstall the app, to remove everything it has stored.

There is no server-side copy to request deletion of.

Children

The app is a vehicle tool and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and the “last updated” date above will change.
