Mimis OBD Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Mimis OBD is an Android application that provides vehicle diagnostics, real-time vehicle information, GPS-based mapping, and trip monitoring. We respect your privacy and aim to protect your data.

1. Information We Collect

Mimis OBD does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or personal account information. The application does not require account registration.

The application accesses vehicle diagnostic information through a connected OBD adapter to display vehicle performance and diagnostic data.

2. Location Information

Mimis OBD uses your device's location to display your position on Google Maps and provide GPS-based speed and trip information.

Mimis OBD does not store GPS coordinates or maintain a separate location database.

When Google Maps is used, location coordinates may be transmitted to Google to display the requested map. Google processes this information according to its own privacy policy.

3. Trip History

Trip history is stored locally on your device. This information may include trip distance, duration, average speed, and vehicle performance data.

You can delete your saved trip history at any time using the application's history-clearing function.

4. Vehicle Diagnostic Information

Mimis OBD reads vehicle information through a compatible OBD adapter, including engine performance, temperature, fuel information, battery voltage, and diagnostic trouble codes.

This information is used to provide vehicle monitoring and diagnostic functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

Mimis OBD uses Google Maps Embed API to display maps. Google may process information associated with map requests, including location data and technical information.

Please review:

Google Privacy Policy

Google Maps Terms of Service

6. Advertising and Analytics

Mimis OBD does not display advertisements and does not integrate advertising or analytics services.

7. Data Storage and Security

Saved trip history is stored locally on your device. Mimis OBD does not operate a remote database for storing your trip history or personal information.

You can remove saved trip information through the application's history-clearing function.

8. Children's Privacy

Mimis OBD is designed for vehicle owners and drivers. It is not specifically directed toward children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy when application features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
